PocketBill Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026 App: PocketBill Platform: Android

PocketBill is a simple, offline-first personal expense and income tracker for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information PocketBill stores on your device, how the app uses third-party advertising, and the choices you have over your data.

1. Introduction

PocketBill ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal finance tracking application for Android. The app is designed to work primarily on your device and does not require account registration. This policy describes the limited data PocketBill handles in order to provide its core functionality and to display advertising.

2. Information stored by PocketBill

PocketBill stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Transaction records you create (type, amount, category, optional note, date)
  • Your theme preference (Light, Dark, or System) for the app's appearance

None of this information is uploaded to any server operated by PocketBill. PocketBill does not have a user-account system and does not synchronize data between devices.

3. Local financial data

Your expense and income records are stored in a local SQLite database file that lives inside the app's private storage on your Android device.

  • The database is created the first time the app launches and is maintained locally.
  • No PocketBill backend receives, processes, or stores your financial records.
  • No transaction record, balance, category, or note is transmitted to PocketBill's own servers (because PocketBill does not operate any such servers).

4. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)

PocketBill uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising. Google Mobile Ads is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

When ads are shown, Google (and AdMob) may process certain information in order to deliver, measure, and improve advertising. This can include a device advertising identifier, coarse location signals, and app-usage information. Google's processing is governed by Google's own privacy policies, not by PocketBill.

  • PocketBill does not intentionally pass your expense, income, category, note, or balance information to the advertising SDK.
  • PocketBill does not control what information Google or its advertising partners collect when an ad is shown.
  • You can review Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising-specific information at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Internet connectivity

PocketBill is designed to work even without an internet connection.

  • Without internet: PocketBill shows no ads. Every core feature — adding an expense, adding an income, viewing history, deleting entries, switching themes, and clearing data — continues to work fully offline.
  • With internet: PocketBill may display ads served by Google Mobile Ads, in addition to all of its offline features.
  • PocketBill does not show any "no internet" warning or block the user from saving or viewing records when offline.

6. Data sharing

PocketBill does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to any third party. The only external data exchange comes from third-party advertising (see Section 4). PocketBill itself does not share financial records with anyone.

7. Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you have full control over its lifetime.

  • Delete a single entry: open the History screen, tap an entry, and choose Delete.
  • Delete every entry: open Settings → Data → "Clear all data" and confirm.
  • Delete everything: uninstall PocketBill. Uninstalling permanently removes the local database file.

PocketBill cannot restore records once they have been deleted or once the app has been uninstalled.

8. Children's privacy

PocketBill is not directed to children under 13 (or the age defined by applicable local law). The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because PocketBill does not collect personal information from any user, no special handling is required.

9. Security

PocketBill stores its SQLite database inside the app's private storage, which is sandboxed by Android and is not accessible to other apps on the device without root access. PocketBill does not transmit financial records to any server, so the surface area for network-based attacks on your financial data is intentionally minimal.

Reminder. No device or app can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping physical access to your device and using Android's built-in screen-lock features.

10. Third-party services

PocketBill uses the following third-party services. Each service has its own privacy practices, which we link to for transparency:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — advertising delivery and measurement. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Flutter — the app framework used to build PocketBill. PocketBill does not send your financial data to Flutter or Google as part of normal app operation.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to PocketBill or to the third-party services it uses. The "Effective date" at the top of this page will be revised whenever an update is published. Material changes may also be communicated through an in-app notice on the Settings screen.
