Privacy

Your money records stay on your device

PocketBill is built around local storage. The app does not sync your data to any PocketBill server.

Local-only data

  • Stored in a local SQLite database on your device
  • No account required
  • No cloud synchronization
  • No PocketBill backend
  • Expense and Income records remain local

You stay in control

  • Delete any entry from the History screen
  • Clear every record from Settings
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes stored data
  • PocketBill does not send your expense and income records to its own servers
  • Read the full Privacy Policy for details
